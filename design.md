# Giri Restaurant - Design System (Logo-Based)

## Overview

This design system is based on the Giri Restaurant logo featuring a chef
hat, crossed spoon & fork, green leaves, and a premium circular badge.
The UI should feel elegant, modern, warm, and welcoming.

## Brand Values

-   Fresh Food
-   Premium Quality
-   Family Dining
-   Fast Service
-   Clean & Hygienic

## Visual Identity

### Logo

-   Primary: Full-color logo
-   Secondary: Monochrome logo

-   SVG icon for web/apps
